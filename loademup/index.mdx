**LoadEmUp** is your go-to plugin manager for safely and efficiently managing plugins on Spigot servers. Designed with stability and flexibility in mind, LoadEmUp empowers server admins and developers to **load**, **unload**, **enable**, **disable**, and **reload** individual plugins — all without restarting your entire server.

This wiki is your comprehensive guide to everything LoadEmUp: from installation and configuration, to using commands and understanding how the plugin safeguards your server with dependency checks, virus scanning, and integrity validation. Whether you're new to plugin management or a seasoned server admin, this resource will help you get the most out of LoadEmUp and keep your Minecraft world running smoothly.

Jump in and discover how to make your plugin workflows safer, smarter, and seamless!
